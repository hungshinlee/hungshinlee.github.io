{{ t.teachingTitle }}

期待學生能全面提升 AI 智商與人文情懷。所謂 AI 智商，是指知道如何與 AI 互動、將 AI 工具用得巧妙，用它補足自己原本不足的知識與能力；而人文情懷，是知道一個系統該為誰而做、在什麼情境下會失效，以及當它出錯時，誰會承擔後果。兩者要一起長大，技術才有意義。

My aim is to raise both my students’ AI intelligence and their humanistic sensibility. By AI intelligence I mean knowing how to work with AI — using these tools deftly and using them to make up for what one does not yet know or cannot yet do. The humanistic side is knowing who a system is for, where it will fail, and who bears the consequences when it does. Technology means something only when the two grow together.
